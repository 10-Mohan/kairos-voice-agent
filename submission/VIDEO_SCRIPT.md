# Kairos Demo Video Script

Target length: 60-75 seconds

1. Opening (0:00-0:08)
   “Sales reps do not lose calls because they lack knowledge. They lose moments because the right response arrives too late.”

2. Product (0:08-0:22)
   “Kairos is a live sales-call coach. The browser captures speech, and the interface keeps the transcript and coach panel visible in the same view.”

3. Demo line (0:22-0:35)
   “A buyer says: Honestly, your pricing feels really high compared with the other vendors we’re evaluating. That finalized sentence is classified as a pricing objection.”

4. Architecture (0:35-0:52)
   “Groq decides whether a moment needs coaching. If it does, Moss searches objections, pricing, battlecards, and deal notes using local sentence-transformer embeddings. Groq turns the best match into one short line and sends it back over WebSocket.”

5. Technical hook (0:52-1:04)
   “The technical hook is Moss: retrieval is measured at roughly 15 to 40 milliseconds, so search becomes a rounding error and the latency budget goes to language generation.”

6. Close (1:04-1:12)
   “Kairos puts the right sentence in front of a rep while the moment still matters.”
